Ventiq data deletion

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Ventiq does not require an account and does not intentionally collect your name, address or phone number. Analytics and attribution providers may process a device advertising identifier and gameplay events.

How to request deletion

  1. Email boka9966@gmail.com with the subject Ventiq Data Deletion.
  2. State whether you use Android or iOS and, if available, include your resettable advertising identifier. Do not send passwords or payment details.
  3. We will confirm receipt, identify the relevant records and request deletion or suppression from the applicable service providers. We aim to complete valid requests within 30 days, subject to legal retention requirements.

You can also stop future collection by uninstalling the game and resetting or limiting the advertising identifier in your device settings.

Удаление данных

Отправьте письмо на boka9966@gmail.com с темой Ventiq Data Deletion. Укажите Android или iOS и, если он доступен, сбрасываемый рекламный идентификатор устройства. Не отправляйте пароли и платёжные данные. Мы подтвердим получение и постараемся выполнить обоснованный запрос в течение 30 дней.
